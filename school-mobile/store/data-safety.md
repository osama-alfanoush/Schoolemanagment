# Play Data Safety declaration

**This is not paperwork.** It declares what children's data the app collects,
and it has to match three things at once: what the code does, what the privacy
policy says, and what the data-processing agreement with the school says. An
inconsistency between them is a compliance problem, not a store problem.

Every row below cites where in the code the behaviour lives. **If you change
one of those files, this document is part of the change.**

Filled in against commit-time behaviour of `school-mobile` and the
`/api/mobile/v1/*` surface.

---

## 1. Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?

**Yes.** The app is a school records client; a family's records are the point
of it.

## 3. Data handling

| Question | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | **Yes** | HTTPS only. The Android network security config forbids cleartext. |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted? | **Yes, through the school** | The school is the data controller; the app is the school's client. Families ask the school, and the school deletes through the admin surface. This must match the DPA wording exactly. |
| Is your app's data collection independently validated against a global security standard? | **No** | Do not claim otherwise. |

## 4. What would make this declaration wrong

Add a row here before shipping any of these, not after:

- **Any analytics or crash-reporting SDK.** Three rows change, and every one of
  them is a "collected" answer this app currently gets to say No to.
- **Collecting a payment method in-app.** Changes Financial info, and pulls in
  Play's payments policy.
- **Surfacing `medical_notes` on any mobile screen.** Health data.
- **Any student-to-student feature.** Not a Data Safety row on its own, but it
  changes the content rating and adds a moderation obligation. Order 6.2
  excludes it deliberately.
- **A push provider** (blocked item 2). An FCM token is a device identifier
  held by a third party, and "Shared" stops being No for it.
