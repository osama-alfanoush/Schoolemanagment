## 1. The one idea that changes everything

Your current positioning is **"a better school management system."** That is a crowded, discretionary purchase. Schools delay it for years.

Your new positioning should be:

> **The only school system in Jordan that issues a JoFotara-cleared fee invoice and collects it through eFAWATEERcom — in Arabic, with the school's accounting closed automatically.**

**Arabic:**

> النظام المدرسي الوحيد في الأردن الذي يُصدر فاتورة الرسوم مُصادَقة من "جوفوترة" ويُحصّلها عبر "إي فواتيركم" — بالعربية، ومع إقفال محاسبي تلقائي.

Why this is the whole strategy:

* Jordan's national e-invoicing system (JoFotara / النظام الوطني للفوترة) has been mandatory for all B2B, B2C and B2G transactions since 1 April 2025 under Amended Billing and Control Regulation No. 2 of 2025. An invoice issued outside JoFotara **is not a valid tax document.**
* The QR code is **not generated by your software** — it is returned by the ISTD after clearance. So any school running Excel or an old local system is technically non-compliant every time it issues a fee receipt.
* Non-compliance risk includes fines and exclusion from government dealings.

That converts your product from *"nice to have"* into *"the school cannot legally invoice without it."*

**Nobody in your competitive set is selling on this.** Classera, Skolera, EduWave and Edu Step Up all sell on LMS and engagement. Orison sells GCC ERP. Excel + a local accountant is the true incumbent. None of them own the sentence above.

## 3. Why \$10,000 is realistic — and how to price for it

### 3.1 The buyer's arithmetic (this is your entire sales pitch)

A private school in Amman with **700 students** at an average annual fee of **JOD 1,500**:


| Line                                             | Value                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| Annual fee revenue                               | \~JOD 1,050,000                            |
| Typical arrears / late collection                | 10–15% → JOD 105,000–157,000 stuck      |
| Recovering just**3 points**of that               | **+ JOD 31,500 cash in the same year**     |
| Cost of one accounting clerk replaced/redeployed | JOD 500 × 12 = JOD 6,000                  |
| Tax exposure avoided from non-cleared invoices   | fines + exclusion from government dealings |
| **Your price**                                   | **\~JOD 7,000 year one (\~\$9,900)**       |

**Return: roughly 4–5× in year one, in cash, not in "productivity."**

That is the only framing that supports \$10,000. Never sell "modules." Sell recovered cash and removed tax risk.

### 3.2 Packaging


| Tier                                                 | Contains                                                                                                                     | Price                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Core — الإدارة**                           | SIS, attendance, grades, timetable, report cards, parent portal                                                              | JOD 0.45 / student / month |
| **Finance — المالية**←*the tier that sells* | Core + fees, installments,**JoFotara clearance**,**eFAWATEERcom collection**, double-entry accounting, closings, audit trail | JOD 0.85 / student / month |
| **Complete — المتكاملة**                   | Finance + HR, payroll, procurement, inventory, library, transport                                                            | JOD 1.20 / student / month |

Plus:

* **Implementation, data migration, training:** JOD 1,200–3,500 one-time (never free — free implementation signals low value and kills your margin).
* **Minimum annual contract:** JOD 3,500 (protects you from tiny schools that consume all your support time).
* **Custom reports / branded parent app / extra branch:** priced separately.

**How \$10,000 lands:** 700 students × JOD 0.85 × 12 = **JOD 7,140** + JOD 2,000 implementation = **JOD 9,140 ≈ \$12,900 in year one**, or a 550-student school on Complete = JOD 7,920 + setup ≈ \$13,900. You can discount to \$10,000 and still be above target.

**Reference point:** Classera publicly advertises packages from roughly \$75–\$150/month for a 50-student selection, which validates per-student recurring pricing in this category — and puts you *below* them on price while being *above* them on finance depth.

### 3.3 Cash timing

Invoice the school **in August**, annually in advance, when they are collecting first-term fees. Never monthly. Monthly billing to schools is how vendors die.

### 3.4 A warning

If "\$10k" means selling the source code once for \$10,000 — **don't.** You would be trading a business that compounds to \$96,000+ ARR at ten schools for one payment, and handing a competitor your codebase. If you need cash now, sell a **perpetual single-school license with a paid annual support contract**, never the repo.

**If you start the code first and the paperwork second, you will finish the code and still be unable to sell.** Send the biller application and the WhatsApp verification before you write a line of the JoFotara adapter.

## 5. 12-week build plan (P0 only — nothing else)

**Weeks 1–2 — Localization core** JOD as first-class currency (kill the USD default in `tableHelpers.tsx`), per-school currency/tax config, Jordanian phone and date formats, Arabic + English invoice templates, Hijri/Gregorian display, sales tax handling with correct treatment of education-sector exemptions.

**Weeks 3–5 — JoFotara adapter (the moat)** UBL 2.1 XML generation, ISTD clearance API, real-time submission before issuance, QR returned by the authority (never generated locally), mandatory field validation including the seven-digit sales tax number, credit and debit notes, retry/queue for outages, immutable archive, per-school credential vault.

**Weeks 4–6 — Payment provider abstraction + eFAWATEERcom** Refactor payments out of the Stripe-only assumption in `services.php` into a provider adapter. Implement eFAWATEERcom bill presentment (push each student's fee installment as a presented bill), payment notification webhook, automatic reconciliation into the ledger, and cash/agent-channel payments. Keep Stripe for GCC/international later.

**Weeks 6–8 — Parent communication** WhatsApp Business API + SMS fallback: fee reminders, cleared-invoice receipt with QR, absence alerts, report card ready. Arabic templates, opt-in/opt-out, delivery status, full audit history. This is the single feature parents notice — and the reason collection improves.

**Weeks 7–9 — Admissions MVP** Public application form, applicant CRM + waitlist, document upload and verification, interview scheduling, offer → acceptance → deposit (paid through the same rails), parent e-consent, re-enrollment / withdrawal / transfer.

**Weeks 9–10 — Production and privacy hardening** Close the blockers your own release notes list: encrypted **off-host** backups with tested restore, error-rate and latency monitoring with alerting, decided retention and erasure policy, named operational owners, verified external providers. Plus a PDPL pack: privacy notice (Arabic), consent records, breach-notification runbook, cross-border transfer position, DPA template, appointed data protection contact.

**Weeks 10–12 — Sellability kit** Data import templates + validator (Excel → system, because every school's data is in Excel), implementation checklist, Arabic training videos and one-page guides, support ticketing + SLA, status page, contract + privacy terms + DPA, customer backup and exit/data-export procedure.

**Explicitly deferred:** native mobile apps (ship a good installable PWA), LMS, question banks, AI, face recognition, gamification, marketplace, bus GPS, canteen POS.

## 6. Go-to-market — 90 days to the first paid reference

**Target profile:** Jordanian private school, **400–1,200 students**, Amman / Irbid / Zarqa, Arabic-medium or bilingual, currently on Excel + separate accounting software + WhatsApp groups, owner-operated or small chain of 2–3 branches. There are over a thousand private school entities in Jordan; you need twenty conversations, not a thousand.

**Who you sell to, in order:**

1. **Owner / board member** — cares about cash, arrears, tax exposure. Signs.
2. **Financial manager / accountant** — cares about JoFotara, closings, audit. Your champion.
3. **Principal** — cares about staff workload. Neutral.
4. Teachers — do not sell to teachers at this stage.

**The opening line (Arabic):**

> «هل فواتير الرسوم عندكم مُصادَقة من جوفوترة؟ بنعمل فحص امتثال مجاني بـ20 دقيقة.»

That question gets you meetings, because most of them do not have a confident answer.

**Days 1–15**

* Send the eFAWATEERcom biller application and WhatsApp verification (see §4).
* Book 20 school meetings. Run the free compliance check. Record: student count, fee revenue, arrears %, current systems, who issues invoices, what breaks in September.
* Sign **3 design partners** at 50% year-one price, full price from year two, in exchange for a named case study, a reference call, and access to their data for migration testing.

**Days 16–45** — build weeks 1–6 above, with a design partner reviewing every Friday.

**Days 46–70** — build weeks 7–10, migrate design partner #1's real data, close the production/privacy blockers.

**Days 71–90** — run a live fee-collection cycle at design partner #1. Measure: invoices cleared, collection rate before vs after, days-to-cash, support tickets, hours saved in the finance office.

**Day 91+** — turn those numbers into a one-page Arabic case study. That single page is worth more than ten more modules. Then close 5 paying schools at full price.

**Channel that beats cold outreach:** local accounting and audit offices already serve dozens of schools and are already being asked "how do I comply with JoFotara?" Give them 15–20% recurring referral commission. They will sell for you.

## 7. Gulf sequencing — do not go early

**Gate: do not enter any Gulf market until you have 5 paying Jordanian schools and 2 renewals.** A renewal is the only proof that matters.

Then, in order:

1. **Kuwait / Oman / Bahrain** (easiest second market) — smaller, less crowded, Tap Payments covers KNET, Benefit and OmanNet. Same product shape, low compliance delta.
2. **UAE** (highest revenue per school) — federal data protection law with consent, correction and cross-border rules; KHDA fee sheets, parent contracts and detailed registration/refund rules in Dubai; structured e-invoicing (not PDFs). Requires a real localization project, so only enter with a funded partner or a signed anchor school.
3. **Saudi Arabia** (largest, hardest) — needs a PDPL compliance program, ZATCA Phase 2 integration, and a local partner/CR. Never position against Noor; provide Noor-compatible import/export and own private-school finance around it.

The architecture lesson: build the **country pack** as a first-class abstraction now — currency, tax engine, e-invoicing adapter, payment adapter, payroll rules, regulator export templates, communication provider. If Jordan is hardcoded, every new country costs you three months. If it's a pack, it costs three weeks.

## 8. What to say no to

Every one of these delays revenue and none of them wins a deal in Jordan in 2026:

AI teaching assistant · automatic question generation · adaptive learning · gamification and badges · face-recognition attendance · educational marketplace · native iOS/Android apps · live bus GPS · canteen POS · full LMS with content authoring.

Classera markets these heavily. Let them. You are not selling to the same buyer.

## 9. Risk register


| Risk                                                      | Impact                          | Mitigation                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| eFAWATEERcom onboarding takes longer than expected        | Blocks the whole pitch          | Start week 1; ship with bank transfer + cash receipt + card as interim, JoFotara clearance still works without it |
| JoFotara API/spec changes                                 | Invoices rejected               | Version the adapter, keep a rejection queue with alerting, subscribe to ISTD notices, never generate QR locally   |
| Design partner never converts to paying                   | No reference                    | Contract the 50% discount as a*paid*pilot with a signed LOI, not a free trial                                     |
| Support load from first schools eats all engineering time | Product stalls                  | Cap at 3 pilots. Ticketing + SLA from day one. Arabic self-serve videos                                           |
| PDPL exposure on children's data                          | Legal + reputational            | Encrypted backups, retention policy, consent records, breach runbook, DPA in every contract                       |
| Single-founder key-person risk                            | Deal blocker for larger schools | Escrow the code, document the runbook, name a second operational contact                                          |
| Price anchored too low by a cheap local competitor        | Margin collapse                 | Never compete on price. Compete on "is your invoice legally valid?"                                               |
